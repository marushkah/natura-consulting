How I work

Responsible by design

Respect for communities is my deeply rooted why. It shapes every tool I recommend.

  1. 01

    People first, then tools

    I start with your mission and the community you serve. Then I choose the AI and tech that fit how your team already works.

  2. 02

    Privacy by default

    Grassroots groups hold sensitive information about their communities. I set clear rules on what stays out of AI tools, and I favor tools that are private and ad-free.

  3. 03

    Plans your team will use

    Every plan I write names owners, dates and costs. The core recommendations of a national strategy I wrote appear in the government’s published 2024 progress report.

  4. 04

    Fair prices for small budgets

    I have been in your seat. I founded a nonprofit that grew to 400+ members, so I publish fixed prices and you know the full cost before we start.

About

ʻIa ora na, I’m Marushka.

I’m a Tahitian-American nonprofit founder and strategist. Respect for the land and its communities is my deeply rooted why. I move to the San Francisco Bay Area in December 2026, when I finish my MBA at INSEAD.

$130Kraised from corporate funders for a nonprofit I founded
$95Kawarded in student scholarships
132indicators in a national SDG strategy I wrote

In 2018 I founded the South Pacific Islander Organization, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that expands access to higher education for Pacific Island students. I served seven years on its board, including two as president. We grew to 400+ members and 100+ volunteers and ran six programs, including mentoring, scholarships and a virtual education summit.

Before that, I worked at Stanford University as a program manager, admissions officer and alumni relations coordinator. I organized 50+ events for a network of 10,000+ alumni and donors. I started my career at Oracle as a solutions consultant for enterprise clients.

I was the first woman of Tahitian descent to graduate from Stanford with a BS. I’m also a mother of two young children, and that is why I care about technology for families and why I’m building Lilliya.
